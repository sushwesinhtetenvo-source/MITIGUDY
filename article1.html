The Effect of Flood in Myanmar 2024

In 2024, there were huge floods in Myanmar because of excessive monsoon rains, floods of rivers, and Typhoon Yagi. These floods started in late June and affected numerous states and regions of Myanmar, including Kachin, Sagaing, Mandalay, Magway, Bago, Kayin, Mon, Tanintharyi, Ayeyarwady, Yangon, and Shan.By September 2024, approximately one million people were affected by floods, according to UN OCHA. As a result of these floods, many families had to leave their houses and relocate to other places. These floods have damaged many houses, roads, bridges, schools, hospitals, and electricity systems. Crops and fields of farmers have been flooded, causing losses in agricultural activities. Moreover, schools have been closed and turned into shelters for victims. In addition, hundreds of people lost their lives or went missing. Many people faced difficulties getting clean water to drink, food, medicines, and sanitation services. Humanitarian organizations, local authorities, and the Myanmar Red Cross have provided different emergency assistance for the affected people, such as food, clean water, medicines, shelters, and financial assistance. In summary, the 2024 floods became a major natural disaster that affected the lives of people, infrastructure, and the economy of the whole country.
