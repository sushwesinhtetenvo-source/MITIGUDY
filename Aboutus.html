About Us

Our reason to create this site

We created this site because we believe that awareness saves lives. Floods can bring great damage to communities, but with the right information, preparation and support, we can reduce the risks and help people stay safe. Our goal is to share useful knowledge, real stories and practical tips so that everyone in Myanmar can be better prepared and more resilient.

Our mission

Our mission is to build a safer and more resilient Myanmar by spreading awareness, sharing reliable information and supporting communities affected by floods. We aim to educate, empower and bring people together so that no one faces the challenges of natural disasters alone.
