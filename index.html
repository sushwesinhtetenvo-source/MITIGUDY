Flood in Yangon

Exploring the Yangon flood

Flooding in Yangon often occurs during the monsoon season due to heavy rainfall and poor drainage systems. It can damage homes, roads, and businesses while disrupting people's daily lives.

Flood in Bago

Exploring the Bago flood

Flooding in Bago Region, Myanmar, is a common natural disaster, especially during the monsoon season when heavy rainfall causes rivers to overflow. Low-lying areas and communities near rivers are particularly vulnerable to flooding. It can damage houses, roads, schools, and farmland, while forcing families to evacuate their homes.

Flood in Ayeyawady

Exploring the Ayeyawady flood

Flooding in Ayeyarwady Region often occurs during the monsoon season due to heavy rainfall and rising river levels. It can destroy homes, damage farmland, and affect people's livelihoods, especially in riverside communities.
